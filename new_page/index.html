    • About me

    Hi,

    I am a student at the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay. Back then, before joining the massive Institute, I knew I loved making things. Perhaps that was the general idea of choosing Civil Engineering over any other disciplines offered, though I already loved tinkering with computers. If I remember correctly, I first made a booting media when I was a sixth-grader. Since then I've been trying out different things, understanding architectures, optimizing services to boost my highly underpowered machine and understanding how a system works.

    My formal introduction to the world of programming was with the basic CS-101 course. With my newfound love for automation and good old passion for tinkering with systems, I decided to pursue my minor in the Computer Science and Engineering Department, taking up the courses related to networks, network security, and Operating Systems.

    I was selected as a Institute System Administrator to maintain Gymkhana, IITB. This gave me a chance to work on actual servers giving me a first-hand experience of dealing with failures (Recently, an entire RAID system failed unexpectedly within a day!!)

    I like to learn. Throw me some new words I'll most likely be busy on my laptop reading about it! I play badminton, I socialize and (though people keep doubting that) I run! In my pastime, you might find me with my laptop looking for rather unconventional places to visit (Bhaad is a beautiful place in Gujarat!) or find me with my kindle reading something related to the Wizarding World.
